# Hackathon Demo Script: FamilyPath

**Theme:** Deep Reasoning for Living Education
**Duration:** ~2 minutes

## 1. Intro (15s)
*Showing the Home Landing Page (Dashboard)*

**Voiceover:**
"Static curricula fail our kids. Random activities feel disjointed. Meet **FamilyPath**."
"We've built the first **Living Curriculum Engine**, powered by **Gemini 3**."

## 2. Deep Reasoning Showcase (45s)
*Navigating to the "Formations" tab. Clicking "Generate New Arc".*

**Voiceover:**
"It usually takes parents hours to plan a Formation Arc. Watch this."

*Action: Select "Sarah (5yo)" and "James (10mo)". Topic: "Kindness".*

**Voiceover:**
"Gemini 3 doesn't just fill slots. It analyzes Sarah's reading level and James's sensory needs to build a personalized roadmap."
"It connects a Bible story about the Good Samaritan to a hands-on 'Band-Aid' activity for James."

*Show the "Reasoning" toggle or tooltip on a generated card.*
"Look at this. It explains *why* this activity works for *both* children simultaneously."

## 3. Daily Anchor & Chat (45s)
*Switching to the "Chat" capability (The "Anchor" view).*

**Voiceover:**
"Every morning, I get a briefing. It's not just a to-do list."

*Action: Chatbot says "Good morning! Here is today's Anchor."*
*Action: Click the "✨ AI Insight" badge to expand the reasoning.*

**Voiceover:**
"I can see exactly how today's hymn connects to our weekly theme."

*Action: Type in Chat: "James is sick today, can we do something quieter?"*

**Voiceover:**
"Life happens. James is sick? No problem."

*Action: Click Regenerate/Send.*
*Action: Show response: "I've swapped the outdoor game for a quiet story time."*

## 4. Closing (15s)
*Back to Dashboard or nice "FamilyPath" logo.*

**Voiceover:**
"Adaptability. Deep Reasoning. A curriculum that lives and breathes with your family."
"This is FamilyPath. Powered by Gemini 3."
