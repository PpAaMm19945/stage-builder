## 1. Philosophy of Evidence

We reject the reduction of a child's soul to a numerical score. Instead, we gather "witness" and "testimony" of their growth in wisdom and stature.

> "Let your light shine before others, so that they may see your good works and give glory to your Father who is in heaven." (Matthew 5:16)

Assessment is not a judgment of value but a verification of stewardship. It answers the question: "Is the child growing in faithfulness?"

**Summary:** Assessment is the collection of qualitative evidence (fruit) demonstrating a child's development, not a quantitative ranking.

## 2. The Portfolio System

The Portfolio is the primary record of education. It is a curated collection of the student's best work, demonstrating mastery and effort.

### Structure
-   **Core Samples:** One piece of best work per subject per quarter (e.g., a math test, an essay, a science diagram).
-   **Creative Works:** Art, recordings of music or recitation, code.
-   **Service Record:** Documentation of service to family, church, or community.
-   **Reflection:** The student's own commentary on what they learned.

### Digital & Physical
The platform supports digital uploads (photos, PDFs, audio) but encourages keeping physical artifacts.

**Summary:** The Portfolio replaces the transcript as the primary document of capability, showcasing actual work rather than abstract grades.

## 3. Milestone Definitions

Milestones are significant achievements that mark the transition between stages or mastery of a major skill. They are "stones of remembrance" (Joshua 4).

### Examples by Stage
-   **Early Years:** "The Reader's Milestone" (reading first book aloud).
-   **Lower Primary:** "The Scribe's Milestone" (cursive handwriting mastery).
-   **Middle School:** "The Logic Milestone" (completing a formal debate).
-   **Upper School:** "The Capstone Milestone" (Senior Thesis defense).

**Verification:** Milestones are verified by the parent and, where appropriate, an external witness (pastor, mentor, teacher).

**Summary:** Milestones are celebrated events that mark genuine achievement and maturity, providing clear goals for the student.

## 4. Parent Observation Logs

The parent, as the primary shepherd, possesses unique insight into the child's heart. The Observation Log captures character and habit formation often missed by academic tests.

### Format
-   **Frequency:** Weekly or Monthly.
-   **Prompts:**
    -   "Where did you see [Child] display patience this week?"
    -   "What struggle did they overcome?"
    -   "How was their attitude during difficulty?"
-   **Domain Focus:** Specifically tracks "Favor with God" and "Favor with Man."

**Summary:** Observation Logs provide a covenantal witness to the child's spiritual and character development.

## 5. AI Summary Templates

AI is used to synthesize data into helpful summaries for the parent, but **never** to issue a final grade or judgment.

### Constraints
-   **Draft Only:** All AI-generated reports are marked "DRAFT - FOR PARENT REVIEW."
-   **Descriptive, Not Prescriptive:** "John completed 80% of math modules" (Fact) vs. "John is good at math" (Judgment).
-   **No "Falling Behind":** AI avoids language of deficiency, focusing instead on "areas for focus."

### Sample Template
> **Subject:** Wisdom & Order (Math)
> **Period:** Oct 2023
> **Activity:** Completed Unit on Fractions.
> **Observation:** Accuracy was high (90%) on addition, but multiplication of fractions requires more practice.
> **Suggested Focus:** Review multiplication tables to support fraction work.

**Summary:** AI summarizes activity data to save parents time but explicitly defers to parental judgment for the final record.

## 6. Language Guide

We use a specific vocabulary to reinforce our theological commitments.

| Prohibited Term | Required Replacement | Reason |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Grade / Score** | **Evidence / Witness** | Avoids reducing child to a number. |
| **Test / Exam** | **Challenge / Demonstration** | Focuses on showing capability, not fear. |
| **Pass / Fail** | **Met / Not Yet Met** | Emphasizes growth mindset and mastery. |
| **Teacher** (for AI) | **Tool / Resource** | AI cannot teach; it can only present info. |
| **Student** | **Learner / Disciple** | Emphasizes the whole-person nature of education. |
| **Deficient** | **Developing** | Avoids labeling the child as broken. |

**Summary:** Our language shapes our culture; we use words that affirm dignity, growth, and hope.
