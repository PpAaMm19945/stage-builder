## 1. Confessional Foundation

Our educational philosophy is not neutral but rooted in the self-attesting authority of Scripture and the Reformed tradition. We affirm that "the fear of the LORD is the beginning of knowledge" (Prov 1:7).

> "Hear, O Israel: The LORD our God, the LORD is one. You shall love the LORD your God with all your heart and with all your soul and with all your might. And these words that I command you today shall be on your heart. You shall teach them diligently to your children..." (Deuteronomy 6:4-7)

> "And Jesus increased in wisdom and stature, and in favor with God and man." (Luke 2:52)

We stand with Van Til, Kuyper, and Bavinck in rejecting the myth of neutrality. All facts are God's facts; therefore, no discipline—whether mathematics or history—can be truly understood apart from Christ, in whom "are hidden all the treasures of wisdom and knowledge" (Col 2:3).

**Summary:** Education is a covenantal duty to enculturate children into the truth of God's world, grounded in Scripture and rejecting secular neutrality.

## 2. Authority Hierarchy

The family is the primary sphere of education ordained by God. The school or platform acts only *in loco parentis* (in the place of the parent) and only by delegated authority.

**The Divine Order:**
1.  **God:** The source of all truth and authority.
2.  **Parents:** The God-ordained shepherds and primary educators (Eph 6:4).
3.  **Curriculum/Church:** The supporting structures and content.
4.  **Tools (AI/Tech):** Servants to the process, never masters.

> "Children, obey your parents in the Lord, for this is right." (Ephesians 6:1)

We explicitly deny AI or any algorithm the moral authority to judge a child or usurp parental decision-making. "AI informs; parents discern."

**Summary:** Parents hold supreme authority under God for their children's education; all tools and curricula serve the parent-child relationship.

## 3. Biblical Anthropology

We view the child through the dual lens of Creation and Fall.
-   **Image Bearer (Imago Dei):** Every child possesses inherent dignity, creativity, and capacity for truth.
-   **Fallen Creature:** Every child inherits a sinful nature (Original Sin) and is prone to folly (Prov 22:15).

> "Folly is bound up in the heart of a child, but the rod of discipline drives it far from him." (Proverbs 22:15)

Education is not merely "drawing out" innate goodness (as in Romanticism) but is *paideia*—the enculturation and discipline required to form a soul. It requires both grace and correction, nourishment and pruning.

**Summary:** Children are created in God's image but require redemptive discipline and instruction to overcome their fallen nature and flourish.

## 4. Telos of Education

The end goal (*telos*) of education is not economic productivity, credential accumulation, or social status. It is the formation of an independent adult who knows, loves, and serves God.

> "The end then of learning is to repair the ruins of our first parents by regaining to know God aright, and out of that knowledge to love him, to imitate him, to be like him..." (John Milton)

We aim for the child to become a "man of God, competent, equipped for every good work" (2 Tim 3:17).

**Summary:** The goal of education is the formation of Christ-like character and the equipping of the child for fruitful service to God and neighbor.

## 5. Learning Pillars (Irreducible Core)

We define the core of a Reformed education not by state standards but by the necessities of a God-centered life.

1.  **Faith Formation:** Scripture, catechism, prayer, and worship. The "milk" and "meat" of the Word.
2.  **Literacy:** The ability to read the Word and the world for truth, and to write with clarity and persuasion.
3.  **Numeracy:** Functional fluency in mathematics to steward creation and reason logically ("thinking God's thoughts after Him").
4.  **History:** Understanding one's place in God's unfolding providence and narrative identity.
5.  **Science:** The study of God's general revelation, fostering wonder, stewardship, and creation care.

**Summary:** The curriculum is anchored in five pillars designed to equip the child for spiritual maturity and earthly stewardship.

## 6. Assessment Philosophy

We reject the industrial model of standardized testing as the primary measure of a soul. Assessment is a form of "witness" or "testimony" regarding a child's growth.

-   **Portfolio of Work:** The primary evidence of learning (essays, projects, recordings).
-   **Milestone Demonstrations:** Public or family presentations of learning.
-   **Parent Observation Logs:** The covenantal witness of the parent regarding character and habit formation.
-   **AI Pattern Analysis:** A servant tool to identify gaps, never a judge of worth.

> "By their fruit you will know them." (Matthew 7:20)

We explicitly reject grades and rankings that foster pride or despair. We value "evidence of growth."

**Summary:** Assessment is a qualitative testimony of a child's faithfulness and growth, relying on portfolios and parental witness rather than impersonal scores.

## 7. Pace and Progression

Education must respect the child's developmental readiness ("milk before meat") while challenging them to grow.

-   **Readiness over Chronology:** A child advances when they have mastered the material, not when they have a birthday.
-   **Parent-Gated Advancement:** Parents control the flow of new material, especially sensitive topics.
-   **"Milk before Solid Food":** We ensure foundational truths are digested before introducing complex abstractions (Heb 5:12-14).

> "Solid food is for the mature, for those who have their senses trained by practice to discern good and evil." (Hebrews 5:14)

**Summary:** Progression is individualized and mastery-based, governed by parental discernment and the child's demonstrated readiness.

## 8. Memorization Doctrine

We affirm the crucial role of memorization in forming the "furniture of the mind." It is not "rote learning" in the pejorative sense but the internalization of truth.

-   **Scripture:** Non-negotiable hiding of God's word in the heart (Psalm 119:11).
-   **Catechism:** Systematizing truth for a coherent worldview.
-   **Poetry & Song:** Forming the moral imagination and the "inner ear" for beauty.
-   **Math Facts:** Achieving fluency to free the mind for higher reasoning.

**Summary:** Memorization is a spiritual discipline that internalizes truth, beauty, and logic, providing the raw material for future wisdom.

## 9. AI Integration Policy

Artificial Intelligence is a powerful tool ("techné") but has no moral agency. It is a tool in the parent's hands.

-   **Role:** AI may explain, summarize, generate questions, and parse constraints.
-   **Prohibitions:** AI must *never* declare mastery, recommend advancement without oversight, or make moral judgments.
-   **Human-in-the-Loop:** All significant assessments and transitions require human (parent) verification.
-   **Theological Ethics:** We adhere to the principles of the Rome Call for AI Ethics: AI serves humanity and must not reduce persons to data.

**Summary:** AI is a subservient tool used to augment parental capacity, never to replace parental authority or judgment.

## 10. Domain Taxonomy (Luke 2:52 Framework)

We reject secular developmental labels in favor of a biblical taxonomy derived from the incarnation.

| Biblical Domain | Secular Equivalent | Focus |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Wisdom & Order** | Cognitive / Math / Logic | Reasoning, numeracy, discernment |
| **Stature & Stewardship** | Physical / Motor | Health, dexterity, creation care |
| **Favor with God** | Spiritual / Liturgical | Worship, prayer, theology, character |
| **Favor with Man** | Social-Emotional | Virtue, service, civility, ethics |
| **Foundations & Patterns** | Pre-Academic / Literacy | Reading, language, habits of mind |

**Summary:** All learning is categorized under the fourfold growth of Christ (Wisdom, Stature, Favor with God, Favor with Man) plus the foundational skills of literacy.
