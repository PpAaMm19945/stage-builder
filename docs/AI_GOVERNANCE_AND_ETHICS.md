## 1. Core Principle: Tool, Not Teacher

Artificial Intelligence is a product of human craft (*techné*). It possesses no soul, no moral agency, and no authority. It is strictly a tool in the hands of the parent (the Shepherd).

> "Shall the axe boast over him who hews with it, or the saw magnify itself against him who wields it?" (Isaiah 10:15)

We reject the anthropomorphizing of AI. It does not "know," "care," or "decide." It processes data.

**Summary:** AI is a servant to the educational mission, never its master or architect.

---

## 2. The Rome Call for AI Ethics

We align our governance with the principles of the [Rome Call for AI Ethics](https://www.romecall.org/), specifically:

1.  **Transparency:** AI systems must be understandable to users. No "black boxes" for critical decisions.
2.  **Inclusion:** AI must not discriminate or bias against any group.
3.  **Responsibility:** There is always a human responsible for the AI's output.
4.  **Impartiality:** AI should not be built to manipulate.
5.  **Reliability:** Systems must be robust and secure.
6.  **Security:** Data privacy is paramount.

**Summary:** We adopt a globally recognized ethical framework that prioritizes human dignity over technological efficiency.

## 3. Prohibited AI Behaviors

To protect the child and the parent's authority, our AI systems are strictly **forbidden** from:

-   **Moral Judgment:** Assessing character, sin, or virtue.
-   **Psychological Profiling:** Diagnosing learning disabilities or mental health conditions.
-   **Automatic Advancement:** Moving a child to a new stage without parental approval.
-   **Theological Innovation:** Generating novel theological doctrines. It must stick to historical confessions.
-   **Usurping Authority:** Commanding the child or contradicting the parent.

**Summary:** AI has hard boundaries; it cannot judge the heart, the mind, or the path of the child.

---

## 4. Permitted AI Uses

We encourage the use of AI to augment parental capacity in the following ways:

-   **Curriculum Adaptation:** Suggesting resources based on learning style.
-   **Drafting & Summary:** Creating summaries of student work for the parent.
-   **Socratic Questioning:** Prompting the student to think deeper (under supervision).
-   **Routine Automation:** Grading simple objective answers (math, spelling) to save parent time.
-   **Resource Retrieval:** Finding scripture, hymns, or historical texts instantly.

**Summary:** AI is powerful for logistics, retrieval, and pattern recognition, freeing parents to focus on relationship and discipleship.

## 5. Human-in-the-Loop Requirements

No consequential decision is made by AI alone.

| Feature | AI Role | Human (Parent) Role |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Assessment** | Drafts report, highlights data. | **Reviews, edits, and signs off.** |
| **Placement** | Suggests level based on quiz. | **Decides and approves level.** |
| **Content Gen** | Drafts lesson plan. | **Verifies theology and appropriateness.** |
| **Coaching** | Suggests schedule adjustments. | **Approves or rejects changes.** |

**Summary:** The "Human-in-the-Loop" is not just a feature; it is a theological necessity to maintain covenantal responsibility.

## 6. Data Privacy and Stewardship

Data is not a commodity; it is a stewardship trust.

-   **No Sale:** We never sell child data.
-   **Minimization:** We collect only what is needed for education.
-   **Parental Ownership:** Parents own their data and can export or delete it at any time.
-   **Isolation:** AI models are not trained on personal user data in ways that leak privacy.

**Summary:** We treat family data with the same sanctity as the family home—private, protected, and under parental rule.
