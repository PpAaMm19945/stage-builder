## 1. Philosophy of Stages

We organize learning not by industrial "grades" but by developmental stages of discipleship. Each stage builds upon the last, moving from concrete foundations to abstract reasoning and fruitful service.

> "When I was a child, I spoke like a child, I thought like a child, I reasoned like a child. When I became a man, I gave up childish ways." (1 Corinthians 13:11)

We use the terminology of craft and apprenticeship:
-   **Novice (Early Years):** Observation and imitation.
-   **Apprentice (Primary/Middle):** Skill acquisition and practice.
-   **Journeyman (Upper):** Independent application and service.

**Summary:** The scope and sequence follows a "Novice → Apprentice → Journeyman" model, respecting the child's developmental maturity.

## 2. Early Years (0-5): The Garden

**Focus:** Habit formation, sensory integration, and the vocabulary of faith.
**Role:** Novice / Observer.

### Capabilities by Domain (Luke 2:52)

| Domain | Capabilities |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Favor with God** | Simple prayers, hymns, Bible stories, "God made this." |
| **Wisdom & Order** | Counting objects, sorting shapes, observing cause-and-effect. |
| **Favor with Man** | "Please" and "Thank you," sharing, obedience to parents. |
| **Stature** | Gross motor play, fine motor grasping, outdoor exploration. |
| **Foundations** | Listening to read-alouds, recognizing letters, oral narration. |

### Sample Activities
-   Nature walks observing God's creation.
-   Singing simple Psalms and hymns.
-   Memorizing short verses (e.g., "God is love").
-   Habit training (e.g., tidying up).

**Evidence of Growth:** Obedience to simple commands, joyful participation in family worship, physical coordination.
**Parent Role:** Constant Shepherd. High involvement.
**AI Role:** None for the child. Parent resource only.

**Summary:** The Early Years focus on establishing habits of obedience and wonder in the "garden" of the home.

## 3. Lower Primary (6-9): The Grammar of Faith

**Focus:** Foundational skills (literacy, numeracy) and memorization of core truths.
**Role:** Apprentice Level 1.

### Capabilities by Domain

| Domain | Capabilities |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Favor with God** | Catechism memorization (Q&A), reciting longer passages. |
| **Wisdom & Order** | Arithmetic fluency, telling time, basic logic of categories. |
| **Favor with Man** | Fairness in play, respecting authority, helping siblings. |
| **Stature** | Team sports, handicrafts, stewardship of body. |
| **Foundations** | Phonics mastery, fluent reading, copywork (penmanship). |

### Sample Activities
-   Narrating chapters of Scripture or history.
-   Drilling math facts (skip counting).
-   Reciting the Apostles' Creed.
-   Copying Proverbs for handwriting.

**Evidence of Growth:** Reading fluency, accurate recitation, self-control in play.
**Parent Role:** Active Teacher. Shoulder-to-shoulder instruction.
**AI Role:** Parent aid for generating drill sheets or explaining concepts to the parent.

**Summary:** Lower Primary builds the "grammar" of learning—the basic facts and skills needed for future reasoning.

## 4. Middle School (10-13): The Logic of Truth

**Focus:** Understanding connections, argumentation, and internalizing the "why."
**Role:** Apprentice Level 2.

### Capabilities by Domain

| Domain | Capabilities |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Favor with God** | Understanding doctrine, personal devotions, sermon note-taking. |
| **Wisdom & Order** | Algebra, formal logic, analyzing historical cause-and-effect. |
| **Favor with Man** | Conflict resolution, service projects, understanding civics. |
| **Stature** | Puberty and stewardship, specialized skills (music, sport). |
| **Foundations** | Essay writing, analyzing literature, research skills. |

### Sample Activities
-   Debating historical decisions from a biblical worldview.
-   Writing persuasive essays.
-   Science experiments with lab reports.
-   Leading a portion of family worship.

**Evidence of Growth:** Logical argumentation, taking responsibility for schedule, articulating personal faith.
**Parent Role:** Coach/Mentor. Guiding from the side.
**AI Role:** Controlled research tool (with guardrails), logic checker.

**Summary:** Middle School transitions to the "logic" stage, where students learn to connect facts and reason biblically.

## 5. Upper School (14-18): The Rhetoric of Service

**Focus:** Expression, persuasion, independent study, and preparation for calling.
**Role:** Journeyman.

### Capabilities by Domain

| Domain | Capabilities |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Favor with God** | Apologetics, leading Bible study, theological integration. |
| **Wisdom & Order** | Advanced math/science, philosophy, financial stewardship. |
| **Favor with Man** | Leadership, vocational apprenticeship, public speaking. |
| **Stature** | Physical fitness discipline, life skills (cooking, repairs). |
| **Foundations** | Rhetoric, original research, senior thesis. |

### Sample Activities
-   Senior Thesis defending a biblical position.
-   Apprenticeship or internship.
-   Leading community service.
-   Teaching younger siblings.

**Evidence of Growth:** Independent management of learning, clear articulation of worldview, service to the church.
**Parent Role:** Advisor/Guidance Counselor. Releasing authority.
**AI Role:** Research assistant, drafting aid (not writer), Socratic partner.

**Summary:** Upper School is the "rhetoric" stage, preparing the student to speak the truth in love and enter the world as a servant-leader.
