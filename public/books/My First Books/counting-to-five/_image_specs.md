# child Counts to Five (and Beyond!)
## Complete Image Specification Document

**Book ID:** `counting-to-five`
**Series:** My First Books
**Target Age:** 2-5 years (24-60 months)
**Domain:** Cognitive (Pre-Math / Number Recognition)
**Page Count:** 10 content pages + 1 cover

---

## BOOK PHILOSOPHY

### Educational Purpose
This book teaches number recognition (1-10) using familiar Ugandan objects. Each page introduces one number with a concrete, countable object that children encounter in daily life. The progression builds confidence—starting with easily recognizable items (banana, hens) and expanding to more exciting concepts (drums, stars).

### Learning Goals
By the end of this book, a child should be able to:
1. Recognize written numerals 1-10
2. Associate numerals with quantities
3. Count objects up to 10
4. Identify common Ugandan household items and animals
5. Build vocabulary in English (with Ugandan cultural context)

### Pedagogical Approach
- **Concrete to Abstract**: Real objects → number concept
- **Repetition with Variation**: Same structure, different objects
- **Interactive Prompts**: "Can you count them?" encourages participation
- **Cultural Grounding**: All objects are locally relevant

---

## MASTER CHARACTER: child

**Full Name:** child  
**Age:** 3-4 years old  
**Personality:** Curious, eager learner, loves discovering new things  
**Appearance:**
- Round, healthy face with bright, curious eyes
- Short natural hair (typical Ugandan boy haircut)
- Warm brown skin (approximately #6B4423)
- Wears simple, clean clothing (t-shirts, shorts)
- Often shown reaching toward objects or pointing

**Role in Book:** child is the "guide" who shows children the objects. His hands appear on several pages reaching toward items, and he appears fully on the cover and final page.

---

## VISUAL CONSISTENCY RULES

### Lighting
All pages use **warm morning light** (golden hour, ~7-8 AM feel) coming from the left side of the frame. This creates:
- Soft shadows to the right
- Warm color temperature
- Inviting, breakfast-time atmosphere

### Background Pattern
**Indoor pages (1-4, 6-7):** Simple Ugandan home interior with:
- Light blue or cream painted cement wall
- Simple wooden furniture
- Occasional window with light

**Outdoor pages (5, 8, 9):** Ugandan compound/landscape with:
- Red earth ground
- Green vegetation
- Blue sky with soft clouds

### Composition
All pages follow **Template A (Object Focus)**:
- Object(s) centered or golden-ratio positioned
- Clean background with minimal distraction
- Bottom 15% left slightly less detailed for text overlay
