# My Colors
## Complete Image Specification Document

**Book ID:** `colors-around-me`
**Series:** My First Books
**Target Age:** 1-4 years (12-48 months)
**Domain:** Cognitive (Color Recognition)
**Page Count:** 10 content pages + 1 cover

---

## BOOK PHILOSOPHY

### Educational Purpose
This book teaches color recognition using familiar East African objects. Each page introduces one color with a concrete, relatable object that children encounter in daily life. The progression builds awareness—starting with primary colors (red, yellow, blue) and expanding to earth tones and mixed colors that fill an African child's visual world.

### Learning Goals
By the end of this book, a child should be able to:
1. Recognize and name 9 basic colors
2. Associate colors with concrete objects from their environment
3. Identify colors in their surroundings ("color hunt" skill)
4. Build vocabulary in English with culturally relevant context
5. Develop visual discrimination skills

### Pedagogical Approach
- **Concrete to Abstract**: Real colored objects → color concept
- **Repetition with Variation**: Same structure ("COLOR like..."), different objects
- **Interactive Prompts**: "What else is...?" encourages application
- **Cultural Grounding**: All objects are locally relevant (African dress, banana leaves, passion fruit)
- **Identity Affirmation**: Brown skin celebrated as beautiful

---

## VISUAL CONSISTENCY RULES

### Lighting
All pages use **warm natural daylight** (morning to afternoon) creating:
- Soft, even illumination
- Natural color accuracy (critical for color learning)
- Warm, inviting atmosphere
- Gentle shadows for depth

### Background Pattern
**Indoor pages (1, 8, 9):** Ugandan home interior with:
- Simple painted walls (cream, light blue)
- Minimal visual clutter
- Focus on the colored object

**Outdoor pages (2, 3, 4, 6, 7):** Natural settings with:
- Sky, grass, or garden elements
- Soft, defocused backgrounds
- The colored object as clear focal point

**Close-up pages (5):** Personal, intimate framing:
- Child's hands/face
- Very simple background (blur)
- Celebrates brown skin

**Finale page (10):** Collection/celebration:
- Multiple colors together
- Joyful, abundant composition

### Composition
All pages follow **Template A (Object Focus)** except finale:
- Colored object centered and prominent
- Clean background with minimal distraction
- Bottom 15% left simpler for text overlay
- Object takes up 30-50% of frame
