# Dedication

For every girl learning to guard what she hears and speaks.

---

# Page 1
![Page 1](images/page-01.png)

Sanyu loves to listen. She is very good at it. She hears EVERYTHING.

The birds in the morning. The water boiling. The voices of grown-ups talking.

---

# Page 2
![Page 2](images/page-02.png)

One evening, Sanyu is supposed to be sleeping. But she hears Maama and Auntie talking outside.

"Mama Tendo is very sick. But she doesn't want people to know..."

Sanyu's ears perk up. Interesting!

---

# Page 3
![Page 3](images/page-03.png)

The next day at church, Sanyu sees her friend Grace.

"Guess what I know!" Sanyu whispers. Her heart beats with excitement.

Grace leans in close.

---

# Page 4
![Page 4](images/page-04.png)

"Mama Tendo is sick! Very sick! Maama said so!"

Grace's eyes grow wide. "Really?!"

Sanyu feels important. She knows something others don't.

---

# Page 5
![Page 5](images/page-05.png)

But Grace tells her mother. And her mother tells another woman. And soon, everyone is whispering.

"Did you hear about Mama Tendo...?"

---

# Page 6
![Page 6](images/page-06.png)

After the service, Sanyu sees Mama Tendo sitting alone on a bench. Her eyes are red. She looks... broken.

People are staring at her. Some are pointing.

Sanyu's stomach twists.

---

# Page 7
![Page 7](images/page-07.png)

In the car, Maama is silent. Her face is serious.

"Sanyu, did you tell someone about Mama Tendo?"

Sanyu's face burns. She looks at her shoes.

"I... I just..."

---

# Page 8
![Page 8](images/page-08.png)

"Some things we hear are not for us to share. Mama Tendo trusted us. Now she is hurt and embarrassed."

Sanyu feels a weight in her chest. It is heavy. It doesn't go away.

---

# Page 9
![Page 9](images/page-09.png)

That night, Sanyu cannot sleep. She keeps seeing Mama Tendo's sad face.

She goes to Mercy's bed. "Mercy... I made a mistake."

Mercy holds her hand.

---

# Page 10
![Page 10](images/page-10.png)

"Words are like seeds, Sanyu. Once you throw them, they grow. You cannot take them back."

Sanyu nods. Her eyes are wet.

"What do I do?"

---

# Page 11
![Page 11](images/page-11.png)

The next week, Maama takes Sanyu to Mama Tendo's house.

Sanyu's hands shake. But she says, "I am sorry. I should not have told."

Mama Tendo hugs her. "I forgive you, little one."

---

# Page 12
![Page 12](images/page-12.png)

Walking home, Sanyu holds Maama's hand tightly.

She thinks about words. About secrets. About the power of her tongue.

Some things are not hers to share.

She is learning.
