# Dedication

For every girl learning to trust when life feels unfair.

---

# Page 1
![Page 1](images/page-01.png)

Today is Grace's birthday party! There will be soda, and cake, and games!

Sanyu wakes up excited—and then she sneezes. Her head hurts. Her body aches.

Oh no.

---

# Page 2
![Page 2](images/page-02.png)

Maama touches Sanyu's forehead.

"You have a fever, little one. You must rest today."

"But... but it's GRACE'S BIRTHDAY!"

---

# Page 3
![Page 3](images/page-03.png)

Sanyu's eyes fill with tears.

"It's not fair! Everyone else gets to go! Why do I have to be sick TODAY?"

She pulls her blanket over her head.

---

# Page 4
![Page 4](images/page-04.png)

She hears Mercy and Baby Joshua getting ready. She hears them laughing.

The door opens and closes.

The house is quiet.

Sanyu feels very, very alone.

---

# Page 5
![Page 5](images/page-05.png)

Then she hears footsteps.

Someone sits on the edge of her bed.

"I'm not going."

It's Mercy.

---

# Page 6
![Page 6](images/page-06.png)

Sanyu peeks out from under the blanket.

"Why not? You LOVE cake."

Mercy smiles. "I love you more. I didn't want you to be by yourself."

---

# Page 7
![Page 7](images/page-07.png)

Sanyu's throat tightens. She doesn't know what to say.

Mercy brought a book. "Do you want me to read to you?"

Sanyu nods.

---

# Page 8
![Page 8](images/page-08.png)

Mercy reads. Her voice is soft. The afternoon light comes through the window.

Sanyu feels sleepy. Safe. Warm.

---

# Page 9
![Page 9](images/page-09.png)

Later, Jjajja brings soup. It smells like home—ginger, onions, love.

"Drink this, my child. It will chase the sickness away."

Jjajja prays quietly over her.

---

# Page 10
![Page 10](images/page-10.png)

By evening, Sanyu's fever is lower.

When Maama and the others return, they bring her a piece of cake wrapped in a napkin.

"Grace saved this for you," Maama says.

---

# Page 11
![Page 11](images/page-11.png)

Sanyu eats the cake slowly. It is sweet.

But something else is sweeter: Mercy stayed. Jjajja prayed. Grace remembered.

She was not forgotten.

---

# Page 12
![Page 12](images/page-12.png)

That night, Sanyu holds Mercy's hand in the dark.

"Thank you for staying."

Mercy squeezes back.

Sometimes the hardest days show us who really loves us.
