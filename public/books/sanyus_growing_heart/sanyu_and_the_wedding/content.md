# Dedication

For every girl learning that true beauty comes from the heart.

---

# Page 1
![Page 1](images/page-01.png)

Everyone is excited! Auntie Rose is getting married today!

Sanyu jumps on her bed. "I get to wear my best dress! I get to see the BRIDE!"

---

# Page 2
![Page 2](images/page-02.png)

Maama braids Sanyu's hair with red beads. Mercy ties a beautiful ribbon—the one she gave Sanyu at the market.

Jjajja sits quietly, putting on her old gomesi dress. It is faded but clean.

---

# Page 3
![Page 3](images/page-03.png)

At the church, Sanyu's eyes grow round as plates.

Auntie Rose walks in. Her dress is white and sparkly. Her skin glows. Everyone gasps.

"She is the MOST beautiful person in the whole world," Sanyu whispers.

---

# Page 4
![Page 4](images/page-04.png)

All through the ceremony, Sanyu stares at Auntie Rose. The dress. The flowers. The makeup.

She thinks: I want to be like that. I want everyone to look at ME.

---

# Page 5
![Page 5](images/page-05.png)

At the reception, Sanyu keeps checking herself in a spoon. Is her hair still nice? Is her dress pretty enough?

She doesn't eat. She doesn't play. She just worries about how she looks.

---

# Page 6
![Page 6](images/page-06.png)

"Sanyu! Come help serve the guests!" Maama calls.

"But I'll get my dress dirty!" Sanyu whines. "I want to stay pretty!"

Maama sighs.

---

# Page 7
![Page 7](images/page-07.png)

Sanyu watches other people serve. Mercy carries plates of food. Maama pours drinks.

And there is Jjajja—old Jjajja—quietly handing out rice to the children. No one is looking at her. But she is smiling.

---

# Page 8
![Page 8](images/page-08.png)

A little boy spills juice on his shirt and cries. Jjajja bends down, wipes his face, and sings softly. He stops crying.

Sanyu watches.

Something about Jjajja seems... beautiful.

---

# Page 9
![Page 9](images/page-09.png)

Later, Mercy sits beside Sanyu.

"Mercy, was Jjajja beautiful when she was young?"

"Oh yes. Jjajja was the most beautiful bride in her village."

Sanyu is surprised. "But now she is old."

---

# Page 10
![Page 10](images/page-10.png)

Mercy smiles. "Her face has changed. But look at her hands—they serve. Look at her eyes—they are kind. That kind of beauty doesn't fade."

Sanyu looks at Jjajja again.

---

# Page 11
![Page 11](images/page-11.png)

On the way home, Auntie Rose hugs everyone goodbye. Her makeup is smudged. Her hair is messy. But she is laughing and crying happy tears.

She still looks beautiful.

But so does Jjajja. And so does Maama.

---

# Page 12
![Page 12](images/page-12.png)

That night, Sanyu looks in the mirror.

She sees her own face—still young.

But she is thinking about Jjajja's kind eyes and Mercy's gentle smile.

Maybe... beautiful isn't just about dresses.
