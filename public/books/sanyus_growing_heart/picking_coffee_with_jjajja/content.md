# Dedication

For every girl learning the joy of working with willing hands.

---

# Page 1
![Page 1](images/page-01.png)

"Sanyu, today you will help Jjajja in the coffee garden."

Sanyu groans. "But Maama, it's so HOT! And my back will hurt!"

Mercy smiles and puts on her sun hat. "I'm coming too."

---

# Page 2
![Page 2](images/page-02.png)

They walk to the garden behind the house. Rows and rows of coffee trees stretch toward the hills.

Jjajja is already there, her basket half full of red coffee cherries.

---

# Page 3
![Page 3](images/page-03.png)

"Pick only the red ones," Jjajja says. "The green ones are not ready."

Sanyu looks at all the trees. So many. She picks one cherry.

"This is going to take FOREVER," she sighs.

---

# Page 4
![Page 4](images/page-04.png)

The sun rises higher. Sanyu's hands are sticky. Her back aches. She has only a small pile of cherries.

"Why do we have to do this, Jjajja? Can't we just BUY coffee?"

Jjajja says nothing. She just keeps picking.

---

# Page 5
![Page 5](images/page-05.png)

Sanyu sits down in a huff. She watches a butterfly. She draws circles in the dirt.

Mercy keeps picking. Jjajja keeps picking.

Nobody stops. Nobody complains.

---

# Page 6
![Page 6](images/page-06.png)

After a long time, Jjajja walks over and sits beside Sanyu.

"Look at my hands, child."

Sanyu looks. Jjajja's hands are rough. Her fingers are bent from years of work.

---

# Page 7
![Page 7](images/page-07.png)

"These hands picked coffee that paid for your mother's school fees. These hands bought the land our house sits on. These hands are never empty."

Sanyu is quiet. She looks at her own soft hands.

---

# Page 8
![Page 8](images/page-08.png)

Jjajja stands up slowly. "A woman who works is not weak. She is strong. Work is not punishment. Work is love."

She goes back to picking.

---

# Page 9
![Page 9](images/page-09.png)

Sanyu watches Jjajja for a long moment. Then she stands up.

She doesn't say anything. She just starts picking again.

One cherry. Then another. Then another.

---

# Page 10
![Page 10](images/page-10.png)

By afternoon, Sanyu's basket is full—really full!

Her back still hurts. Her hands are tired. But something else is different.

She feels... proud.

---

# Page 11
![Page 11](images/page-11.png)

At home, Jjajja spreads the coffee cherries on mats to dry in the sun.

"These will become money for your school fees, just like before," Jjajja says quietly.

Sanyu helped make that happen.

---

# Page 12
![Page 12](images/page-12.png)

That night, Sanyu looks at her hands before bed.

Still small. Still soft.

But today, they did something real.

She holds them close and whispers, "A woman's hands are never empty."
