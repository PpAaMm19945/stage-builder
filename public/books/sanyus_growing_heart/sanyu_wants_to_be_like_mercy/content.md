# Dedication

For every girl learning to become who God made her to be.

---

# Page 1
![Page 1](images/page-01.png)

Sanyu watches Mercy all the time now.

How Mercy speaks gently, even when she is tired.
How Mercy helps without being asked.
How Mercy prays before bed, quietly, with closed eyes.

---

# Page 2
![Page 2](images/page-02.png)

"I want to be JUST like Mercy," Sanyu thinks.

So she tries.

She walks like Mercy—slow and graceful. She talks like Mercy—soft and calm.

---

# Page 3
![Page 3](images/page-03.png)

At breakfast, Sanyu sits very straight.

"Would you like more tea, Maama?" she asks in her most Mercy-like voice.

Maama looks confused. "Are you feeling alright, Sanyu?"

---

# Page 4
![Page 4](images/page-04.png)

At school, Sanyu tries to be patient like Mercy.

But when a boy takes her pencil, she ALMOST yells—then remembers and forces a smile.

It feels fake. Tight. Wrong.

---

# Page 5
![Page 5](images/page-05.png)

At home, she tries to serve like Mercy.

But she spills the water. She burns the porridge. She forgets to feed the chickens.

"This is TOO HARD!" she cries.

---

# Page 6
![Page 6](images/page-06.png)

She throws herself on her bed.

"I'll NEVER be like Mercy. She makes it look easy. I can't do anything right."

Tears fall onto her pillow.

---

# Page 7
![Page 7](images/page-07.png)

A knock at the door. Mercy comes in and sits on the edge of the bed.

"Sanyu, why have you been acting so strange today?"

Sanyu sniffles. "Because... I want to be like you. But I can't."

---

# Page 8
![Page 8](images/page-08.png)

Mercy laughs—not a mean laugh, a kind one.

"You don't have to BE me, Sanyu. God didn't make two Mercys. He made one Mercy and one Sanyu."

---

# Page 9
![Page 9](images/page-09.png)

"But you're so good at everything. You're gentle and patient and kind..."

Mercy shakes her head. "I wasn't always. Do you think I was born this way? I had to learn. I'm still learning."

---

# Page 10
![Page 10](images/page-10.png)

Sanyu is quiet.

"Really?"

"Really. I was loud once. Impatient. I made many mistakes." Mercy smiles. "Just like you."

---

# Page 11
![Page 11](images/page-11.png)

"So... how did you change?"

Mercy thinks. "I didn't copy someone else. I asked God to change my heart. Little by little. Day by day. That is what makes a woman beautiful—not pretending, but becoming."

---

# Page 12
![Page 12](images/page-12.png)

Sanyu looks at her own hands. Her messy hair. Her dirty dress.

"I'm still loud. I still make mistakes."

Mercy holds her hand. "And you are still learning. That's the journey."

---

# Page 13
![Page 13](images/page-13.png)

That night, Sanyu doesn't try to pray like Mercy.

She closes her eyes and prays in her own words—messy, honest, real.

"God, I don't know how to be good. Please help my heart grow."

---

# Page 14
![Page 14](images/page-14.png)

Outside, the moon shines over Namasagali village.

Inside, a little girl is becoming something new.

Not a copy. Not pretending.

Just Sanyu. With a growing heart.
