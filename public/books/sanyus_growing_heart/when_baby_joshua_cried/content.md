# Dedication

For every girl learning to serve even when it is hard.

---

# Page 1
![Page 1](images/page-01.png)

Baby Joshua is crying. Again.

His little face is hot. His nose is runny. Maama has been holding him all day.

Sanyu covers her ears.

---

# Page 2
![Page 2](images/page-02.png)

"Maama, can you braid my hair?"

"Not now, Sanyu. Joshua is sick."

"Maama, can we read a story?"

"Later, Sanyu. I need to give him medicine."

---

# Page 3
![Page 3](images/page-03.png)

Sanyu stomps her foot. "You NEVER have time for me anymore! It's always Joshua, Joshua, JOSHUA!"

She runs to her room and slams the door.

---

# Page 4
![Page 4](images/page-04.png)

Inside, she crosses her arms. Her face is hot.

"Nobody cares about me," she whispers. "The baby ruined everything."

---

# Page 5
![Page 5](images/page-05.png)

At night, Joshua cries and cries. Sanyu can't sleep.

She opens her door a crack and looks out.

Maama is exhausted—her head is dropping. She can barely keep her eyes open.

---

# Page 6
![Page 6](images/page-06.png)

Then Sanyu sees Mercy get up quietly from her mat.

Mercy walks to Maama and takes Joshua gently.

"Go sleep, Maama. I will hold him."

---

# Page 7
![Page 7](images/page-07.png)

Mercy sits by the lamp, rocking the baby. She hums softly. Her eyes are tired, but she doesn't complain.

Sanyu watches from the shadows.

---

# Page 8
![Page 8](images/page-08.png)

Mercy doesn't look angry. She doesn't stomp or slam doors.

She is tired. But she is... peaceful.

How can she be peaceful?

---

# Page 9
![Page 9](images/page-09.png)

The next morning, Sanyu wakes early.

She finds Mercy asleep in the chair, still holding Joshua. His fever has broken. He is smiling.

---

# Page 10
![Page 10](images/page-10.png)

Sanyu looks at her big sister. Dark circles under her eyes. Hair messy. Dress wrinkled.

But she looks... beautiful.

Different from the wedding kind of beautiful. A quieter kind.

---

# Page 11
![Page 11](images/page-11.png)

Sanyu tiptoes to the kitchen. She pours water into a cup.

Quietly, carefully, she brings it to Mercy.

"Thank you," Mercy whispers, surprised.

---

# Page 12
![Page 12](images/page-12.png)

It is a small thing. Just a cup of water.

But Sanyu's heart feels lighter than it has in days.

Maybe serving isn't so bad.

Maybe it's even... good.
