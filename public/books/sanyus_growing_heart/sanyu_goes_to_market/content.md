# Dedication

For every little girl learning to find joy in what she has.

---

# Page 1
![Page 1](images/page-01.png)

The morning is bright and warm. Sanyu bounces out of bed. Today is market day!

"Maama! Maama! Are we going NOW?"

---

# Page 2
![Page 2](images/page-02.png)

Maama laughs and ties her headscarf. Mercy, Sanyu's big sister, puts on her sandals quietly.

"Yes, little one. We are going to Jinja market. But first, breakfast."

---

# Page 3
![Page 3](images/page-03.png)

Sanyu can barely eat her porridge. She wiggles in her seat. She swings her feet.

"Sit still," Maama says gently. "The market will wait for you."

But Sanyu doesn't want to wait.

---

# Page 4
![Page 4](images/page-04.png)

They walk down the dusty road together. Sanyu runs ahead, then runs back. Mercy walks calmly beside Maama, carrying a basket.

The market sounds grow louder. Voices. Colors. Smells.

---

# Page 5
![Page 5](images/page-05.png)

SO MANY THINGS! Tomatoes piled high like mountains. Fabric bright as rainbows. Chickens clucking in cages.

Sanyu's eyes grow wide. She wants to touch EVERYTHING.

---

# Page 6
![Page 6](images/page-06.png)

Then she sees them.

Hair ribbons. Beautiful, silky, shiny hair ribbons. Pink ones. Yellow ones. Blue ones with sparkles.

Sanyu stops. Her heart beats fast.

---

# Page 7
![Page 7](images/page-07.png)

"Maama! Maama! Look! I NEED those ribbons! Please! Please please please!"

Maama shakes her head. "We came for flour and beans, Sanyu. Not ribbons."

"But—but—" Sanyu's lip begins to tremble.

---

# Page 8
![Page 8](images/page-08.png)

Sanyu pouts. She drags her feet. She sighs loudly—VERY loudly—so everyone can hear.

"This isn't fair," she mumbles.

Mercy looks at her but says nothing.

---

# Page 9
![Page 9](images/page-09.png)

They buy the flour. They buy the beans. Sanyu doesn't help. She is too busy being sad about the ribbons.

Then Mercy walks away quietly. Sanyu doesn't notice.

---

# Page 10
![Page 10](images/page-10.png)

On the walk home, Mercy slips something into Sanyu's hand.

A small yellow ribbon. Soft and shiny.

"For you," Mercy whispers. "I saved my coins for weeks. I wanted you to have something beautiful."

---

# Page 11
![Page 11](images/page-11.png)

Sanyu stares at the ribbon. She looks at Mercy's kind eyes.

Something warm fills her chest. It is not the ribbon making her happy. It is something else.

Mercy gave. And giving made Mercy smile.

---

# Page 12
![Page 12](images/page-12.png)

That night, Sanyu holds the ribbon close.

But she is thinking about Mercy's face—peaceful, joyful, content.

Maybe... maybe there is something better than getting things.

Sanyu doesn't have the words yet. But her heart is learning.
