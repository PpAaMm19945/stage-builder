# Dedication

For every girl learning to be kind even when others are not.

---

# Page 1
![Page 1](images/page-01.png)

Sanyu runs to the big tree where the village girls always play.

But when she gets there, they are already laughing—in a circle, without her.

---

# Page 2
![Page 2](images/page-02.png)

"Can I play?" Sanyu asks.

The girls look at each other. One of them whispers something.

"We're in the middle of a game. Maybe later."

They giggle and turn away.

---

# Page 3
![Page 3](images/page-03.png)

Sanyu stands still. Her face burns. Her fists clench.

LATER? They always say later. But later never comes.

Something hot and angry rises in her chest.

---

# Page 4
![Page 4](images/page-04.png)

She wants to yell. She wants to say mean things back.

"FINE! I didn't want to play with you ANYWAY! You're all BORING!"

The girls stare. One of them rolls her eyes.

---

# Page 5
![Page 5](images/page-05.png)

Sanyu runs away, tears streaming.

She hides behind the coffee bushes and cries until her eyes hurt.

It's not fair. Why don't they like her?

---

# Page 6
![Page 6](images/page-06.png)

A shadow falls across her.

It's Mercy. Of course it's Mercy.

She sits down without saying anything.

---

# Page 7
![Page 7](images/page-07.png)

After a long silence, Mercy speaks.

"When I was your age, there was a girl who never let me join. I was so angry, I said something terrible to her. I felt good for a moment. But then I felt worse."

---

# Page 8
![Page 8](images/page-08.png)

"What did you do?" Sanyu sniffles.

"I learned that hard words make more enemies. Soft words—even when people are unkind—leave the door open."

---

# Page 9
![Page 9](images/page-09.png)

Sanyu wipes her face. "But I already yelled at them. What do I do now?"

Mercy smiles gently. "You can't control them. But you can control you. Next time... try soft."

---

# Page 10
![Page 10](images/page-10.png)

The next day, Sanyu sees the girls again.

Her heart beats fast. She takes a breath.

"Can I play today? I'm sorry I yelled yesterday."

---

# Page 11
![Page 11](images/page-11.png)

The girls look surprised. One of them shrugs. "Okay. You can be on my team."

It's not perfect. But it's a door. A small, open door.

---

# Page 12
![Page 12](images/page-12.png)

That night, Sanyu lies in bed.

Her heart still remembers the hurt. But it also remembers the open door.

Soft words. Even when it's hard.

She is learning.
