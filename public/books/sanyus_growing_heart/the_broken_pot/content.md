# Dedication

For every girl learning that the truth, though hard, sets us free.

---

# Page 1
![Page 1](images/page-01.png)

Jjajja has a special clay pot. It sits on a high shelf, round and beautiful.

"Jjajja's mother gave her that pot," Maama says. "It is very, very old."

---

# Page 2
![Page 2](images/page-02.png)

Sanyu knows she is not supposed to play inside. But it's raining. And the ball is bouncing so nicely...

Bounce. Bounce. CRASH.

---

# Page 3
![Page 3](images/page-03.png)

The pot lies in pieces on the floor.

Sanyu's heart stops. Her hands shake. The room is silent except for the rain.

What has she done?

---

# Page 4
![Page 4](images/page-04.png)

She hears footsteps.

Quickly, she sweeps the pieces under the shelf. She puts a cloth over the empty spot.

When Mercy walks in, Sanyu pretends to read a book.

---

# Page 5
![Page 5](images/page-05.png)

That night, Jjajja notices.

"Where is my pot?" Her voice is confused, not angry. That makes it worse.

Sanyu's heart pounds. "I... I don't know."

---

# Page 6
![Page 6](images/page-06.png)

Maama looks around. Mercy looks at Sanyu.

"Did you see anything, Sanyu?"

"No." The word sticks in her throat like a stone.

---

# Page 7
![Page 7](images/page-07.png)

That night, Sanyu can't sleep.

The lie presses on her chest like a heavy blanket.

She closes her eyes, but she sees the broken pieces.

---

# Page 8
![Page 8](images/page-08.png)

The next day feels long. Every time Jjajja speaks, Sanyu flinches.

She tries to play. She can't.

She tries to eat. She can't.

---

# Page 9
![Page 9](images/page-09.png)

Finally, she can't hold it anymore.

She walks to Jjajja, hands behind her back, eyes on the floor.

"Jjajja... I broke your pot. I was playing inside. I'm sorry. I lied."

---

# Page 10
![Page 10](images/page-10.png)

Jjajja is quiet for a long moment.

Then she reaches out and pulls Sanyu close.

"The pot can be replaced, child. But an honest heart? That is a treasure. I am proud of you for telling the truth."

---

# Page 11
![Page 11](images/page-11.png)

Sanyu cries into Jjajja's dress. But these are not heavy tears. These are light tears.

The stone in her chest is gone.

She feels free.

---

# Page 12
![Page 12](images/page-12.png)

Later, Sanyu helps Jjajja glue the pot back together.

It has cracks now. But Jjajja smiles.

"Now it tells a story. The story of a girl who chose truth."
