# Dedication

For every girl learning to open her home with a joyful heart.

---

# Page 1
![Page 1](images/page-01.png)

"Visitors are coming! Pastor Mulongo and his wife, all the way from Kampala!"

The house is suddenly very busy. Maama is cooking. Jjajja is cleaning. Even Taata is sweeping the compound.

---

# Page 2
![Page 2](images/page-02.png)

Sanyu watches everyone rush around.

"Can I go play at Grace's house?" she asks.

"Not today," Maama says. "We need your help."

Sanyu sighs loudly.

---

# Page 3
![Page 3](images/page-03.png)

"Sanyu, sweep the veranda. Sanyu, fold the napkins. Sanyu, chase the chickens out of the sitting room!"

So many instructions! Sanyu doesn't want to do any of them.

She wants to PLAY.

---

# Page 4
![Page 4](images/page-04.png)

She hides behind the banana trees. Maybe no one will find her.

But Mercy finds her.

"Sanyu, why are you hiding?"

"Because I don't want to work! I don't even KNOW these people!"

---

# Page 5
![Page 5](images/page-05.png)

Mercy sits beside her.

"Do you remember when we visited Uncle in Kampala? And Auntie Esther gave you that special juice, and let you sleep in the soft bed?"

Sanyu nods.

---

# Page 6
![Page 6](images/page-06.png)

"She didn't know you either. But she served you with joy. That is what women do. We make people feel welcome."

Sanyu is quiet.

---

# Page 7
![Page 7](images/page-07.png)

The car arrives! A tall man and a kind-looking woman step out.

Everyone greets them warmly. Jjajja bows and shakes their hands.

Sanyu watches from the doorway.

---

# Page 8
![Page 8](images/page-08.png)

During the meal, Maama serves Pastor Mulongo's wife first. Jjajja pours water for the guests. Mercy brings extra plates.

Sanyu notices something strange: they are all smiling. Really smiling.

---

# Page 9
![Page 9](images/page-09.png)

The pastor's wife turns to Sanyu.

"You must be Sanyu. Your grandmother has told me so much about you!"

Sanyu feels seen. Important. Welcome.

---

# Page 10
![Page 10](images/page-10.png)

After dinner, Sanyu quietly helps clear the plates.

Maama catches her eye and smiles. A warm, proud smile.

Sanyu's chest feels full.

---

# Page 11
![Page 11](images/page-11.png)

When the visitors leave, they hold Maama's hand.

"Thank you for such a wonderful welcome. We felt like family."

Sanyu hears those words. Family.

---

# Page 12
![Page 12](images/page-12.png)

That night, Sanyu lies in bed thinking.

Making someone feel welcome... making them feel like family...

Maybe that is a kind of gift too.

A gift that women give.
