# Community Map Guide - Working Fathers of Soroti

This document provides specifications for generating the community map illustration that will be used across the series.

## Setting: Soroti District, Eastern Uganda

### Geography Overview
The map should show Soroti Town as the central hub with surrounding villages connected by roads.

---

## Map Elements

### Soroti Town (Center)
The main trading town - modern, thriving, and well-organized:

1. **Soroti Hospital** - Where Mr. Kato works
   - Multi-story white building with green rooftop
   - Located on the northern edge of town
   - Flag of Uganda flying

2. **Mr. Okello's Garage** - Mechanic workshop
   - Located near Main Street
   - Corrugated iron roof
   - A few cars and motorcycles parked outside

3. **Mr. Mukasa's Shop** - General merchandise store
   - On Main Street
   - Painted storefront with "MUKASA GENERAL STORE" sign
   - Colorful goods visible in window

4. **Soroti Primary School** - Where Mr. Wasswa teaches
   - Yellow-painted building with classrooms
   - Ugandan flag
   - Children playing in the compound

5. **Police Station** - Where Mr. Odong works
   - Blue and white building
   - Police vehicles parked outside

6. **Central Market** - The trading heart
   - Multiple stalls with colorful tarps
   - Farmers bringing produce from villages
   - Busy with activity

7. **Grace Community Church** - Where Pastor Omoding serves
   - Prominent location, possibly on a slight hill
   - White walls with red roof
   - Cross on top
   - Bell tower
   - Central to the community

8. **Army Barracks** - Where Mr. Emukule serves
   - Edge of town
   - Green buildings
   - Flagpole with Uganda flag

9. **Boda-Boda Stage** - Where Mr. Okwaro waits
   - Near the market
   - Several motorcycles parked
   - A shelter for drivers

---

### Surrounding Villages (Connected by Roads)

1. **Katakwi Village** (North)
   - Where Mr. Opio's farm is located
   - Green fields of maize and beans
   - Simple but well-built homes
   - The Opio family home marked

2. **Serere Village** (South)
   - Agricultural area
   - More farms and gardens

3. **Amuria Village** (East)
   - Pastoral area
   - Some families travel here for work

4. **Ngora Village** (West)
   - Where some church members come from
   - Connected by main road

---

### Roads
- **Main Road** - Tarmac road running through Soroti town
- **Village Roads** - Red murram roads connecting villages to town
- **Paths** - Walking paths between homes

## Visual Style
- **Illustrated map style** - Not photorealistic
- **Warm, inviting colors** - Golden yellows, greens, browns
- **Clear labels** - Simple, child-friendly fonts
- **Day time** - Sunny sky with a few clouds
- **Modern Uganda** - Proper buildings, vehicles, infrastructure
- **NO poverty stereotypes** - Well-maintained homes, clean streets, thriving community

---

## Technical Specifications
- **Format**: PNG
- **Aspect Ratio**: 16:9 (landscape) for display, or square for alternate uses
- **Resolution**: Minimum 2048px wide
- **Color Profile**: sRGB
