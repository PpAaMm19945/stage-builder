# Dedication

For all the boda-boda riders who help people get where they need to go.

---

# Page 1
![Page 1](images/page-01.png)

Mr. Okwaro checks his motorcycle. Is there enough petrol? Are the tires good? A boda-boda rider must be ready.

**Ask:** Have you ever ridden on a boda-boda?

---

# Page 2
![Page 2](images/page-02.png)

Mrs. Okwaro gives him a thermos of tea. "Be safe today." Little Apio waves. "Bye-bye, Papa!"

**Ask:** How do you say goodbye?

---

# Page 3
![Page 3](images/page-03.png)

Mr. Okwaro rides to the stage near the market. Other boda-boda riders are waiting too. They greet each other.

**Ask:** Where do taxis wait in your town?

---

# Page 4
![Page 4](images/page-04.png)

A man comes running. "I need to get to the hospital quickly!" Mr. Okwaro starts his engine. "I will take you."

**Ask:** Why do people need to travel fast sometimes?

---

# Page 5
![Page 5](images/page-05.png)

He rides carefully through the streets. Not too fast, not too slow. Safety comes first.

**Ask:** Why is it important to be careful on the road?

---

# Page 6
![Page 6](images/page-06.png)

At the hospital, the man pays and says, "Thank you!" He runs inside to see Mr. Kato, the doctor.

**Ask:** Who helped the man get to the hospital?

---

# Page 7
![Page 7](images/page-07.png)

Next, a mother needs to take her children to school. Mr. Okwaro takes two trips. He is helpful.

**Ask:** How many trips did Mr. Okwaro make?

---

# Page 8
![Page 8](images/page-08.png)

By midday, Mr. Okwaro has earned enough money to buy food for his family. He puts the money safely in his pocket.

**Ask:** Where do you keep your money safe?

---

# Page 9
![Page 9](images/page-09.png)

In the afternoon, he takes Mr. Opio back to his village. The village road is bumpy, but Mr. Okwaro knows the way.

**Ask:** What kinds of roads have you seen?

---

# Page 10
![Page 10](images/page-10.png)

The sun is setting. Mr. Okwaro rides home. He is tired but thankful. Today was a good day.

**Ask:** What makes a day good for you?

---

# Page 11
![Page 11](images/page-11.png)

At home, Apio climbs on his motorcycle pretending to drive. "Vroom vroom!" Mr. Okwaro laughs and hugs her.

**Ask:** What do you pretend to do?

---

# Page 12
![Page 12](images/page-12.png)

On Sunday, Mr. Okwaro gives free rides to elders going to church. "Bless you, son," they say. He smiles at Pastor Omoding.

**Ask:** How do you help others?
