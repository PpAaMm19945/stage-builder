# Image Specifications: Mr. Kato Goes to Work

## Universal Style Prompt (Use at start of EVERY image)
```
A cartoon illustration in a friendly, warm art style with bold outlines and soft coloring.
The scene depicts modern, flourishing community life in Soroti, Uganda.
All buildings have plastered painted walls, glass windows, and iron sheet roofs.
People wear modern, colorful clothing. Technology reflects 2026 Uganda.
```

---

## Character Reference: Dr. Kato
- **Age:** Mid-30s
- **Build:** Medium, professional posture
- **Clothing:** Clean white doctor's coat over collared shirt, stethoscope around neck
- **Expression:** Kind, focused, professional
- **Transport:** Rides a motorcycle (red Honda or similar)
- **Family:** Wife (Mrs. Kato) in colorful gomesi/kitenge, son Tendo (age 4)
