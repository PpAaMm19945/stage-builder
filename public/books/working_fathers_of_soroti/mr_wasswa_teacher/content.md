For all the teachers who shape young minds with patience and love.

![Page 1](images/page-01.png)

The school bell will ring soon. Mr. Wasswa gathers his books. He is a teacher.

**Ask:** What do you bring to school?

---

# Page 2
![Page 2](images/page-02.png)

Mrs. Wasswa hands him his lunch. "Teach well, my husband." The twins, Kato and Nakato, hug him goodbye.

**Ask:** Do you bring lunch to school?

---

# Page 3
![Page 3](images/page-03.png)

Mr. Wasswa walks to Soroti Primary School. The building is painted yellow. Children are arriving.

**Ask:** What color is your school?

---

# Page 4
![Page 4](images/page-04.png)

"Good morning, Teacher!" the children say. Mr. Wasswa smiles. "Good morning, class. Let us learn today."

**Ask:** How do you greet your teacher?

---

# Page 5
![Page 5](images/page-05.png)

First, they practice letters. A, B, C, D... Mr. Wasswa writes on the board. The children copy carefully.

**Ask:** Can you write your name?

---

# Page 6
![Page 6](images/page-06.png)

Then, numbers. "One plus two equals...?" A small hand goes up. "Three, Teacher!" "Very good!"

**Ask:** What is one plus one?

---

# Page 7
![Page 7](images/page-07.png)

One child is struggling. Mr. Wasswa sits beside him. "Try again. Slowly. You can do it." He is patient.

**Ask:** What do you do when something is hard?

---

# Page 8
![Page 8](images/page-08.png)

At break time, children play outside. Mr. Wasswa watches. He loves to see them happy and healthy.

**Ask:** What do you play at school?

---

# Page 9
![Page 9](images/page-09.png)

In the afternoon, Mr. Wasswa reads a story. The children listen quietly. Stories teach us many things.

**Ask:** What is your favorite story?

# Page 10
![Page 10](images/page-10.png)

The school bell rings again. Time to go home. "Goodbye, Teacher!" "Goodbye, children. Study well!"

**Ask:** What do you say when school ends?

# Page 11

**Ask:** Where do you do your homework?
