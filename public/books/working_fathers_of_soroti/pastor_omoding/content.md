# Dedication

For all the pastors who shepherd God's people with love and faithfulness.

---

# Page 1
![Page 1](images/page-01.png)

The sun rises over Soroti. Pastor Omoding is already awake. He reads his Bible and prays. "Lord, help me care for Your people today."

**Ask:** Do you pray in the morning?

---

# Page 2
![Page 2](images/page-02.png)

Mrs. Omoding brings him tea. Little Esther and young Timothy eat their breakfast. The whole family loves Sunday mornings.

**Ask:** What is special about Sunday in your home?

---

# Page 3
![Page 3](images/page-03.png)

Pastor Omoding walks to the church. The building is painted white with a red roof. A cross stands on top.

**Ask:** What does your church look like?

---

# Page 4
![Page 4](images/page-04.png)

The church bell rings. Families come from everywhere. Here come the Katos! And the Okellos! And the Opios on their bicycles from the village!

**Ask:** How does your family get to church?

---

# Page 5
![Page 5](images/page-05.png)

Mr. Mukasa sits with Mr. Wasswa. Mr. Odong, still tired from a long week, sits with Mr. Ejoku. All the fathers are here with their families.

**Ask:** Who sits near your family at church?

---

# Page 6
![Page 6](images/page-06.png)

Mr. Okwaro helps old grandmothers find their seats. Mr. Emukule, in his best clothes, holds little Okello's hand.

**Ask:** How do you help people at church?

---

# Page 7
![Page 7](images/page-07.png)

Pastor Omoding stands before everyone. He opens his Bible. "Today I will teach you about working faithfully."

**Ask:** What do you learn at church?

---

# Page 8
![Page 8](images/page-08.png)

"Some of you are doctors. Some are farmers. Some are builders," Pastor says. "All good work honors God when we do it honestly."

**Ask:** What kind of work do people in your family do?

---

# Page 9
![Page 9](images/page-09.png)

The children have Sunday School. Mrs. Wasswa teaches them songs. "Jesus loves me, this I know..." Tendo and Acen sing loudly.

**Ask:** What songs do you sing at church?

---

# Page 10
![Page 10](images/page-10.png)

After church, Pastor Omoding shakes hands with everyone. "How is your mother, Mr. Kato?" "How is business, Mr. Mukasa?" He knows every family.

**Ask:** Does your pastor know your name?

---

# Page 11
![Page 11](images/page-11.png)

In the afternoon, Pastor Omoding visits a sick neighbor. He prays with them. He brings food from his own table.

**Ask:** How do you help someone who is sick?

---

# Page 12
![Page 12](images/page-12.png)

At home, the Omoding family eats lunch together. Pastor is a father too. He plays with Esther and teaches Timothy to read.

**Ask:** What do you do with your family after church?

---

# Page 13
![Page 13](images/page-13.png)

As the sun sets, Pastor Omoding looks over Soroti town. He sees the hospital, the market, the school, the farms. He loves this community.

**Ask:** What do you love about your town?

---

# Page 14
![Page 14](images/page-14.png)

All the fathers work hard. Mr. Kato heals. Mr. Okello fixes. Mr. Opio grows. Mr. Mukasa sells. Mr. Odong protects. Mr. Wasswa teaches. Mr. Ejoku builds. Mr. Okwaro carries. Mr. Emukule defends. And Pastor Omoding shepherds them all.

**Ask:** Which father is your favorite?
