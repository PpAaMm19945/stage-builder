# Dedication

For all the fathers who run businesses to serve their communities.

---

# Page 1
![Page 1](images/page-01.png)

The morning is cool. Mr. Mukasa walks to his shop on Main Street. It is time to open.

**Ask:** What shops are near your home?

---

# Page 2
![Page 2](images/page-02.png)

He unlocks the door. Inside are many things. Rice, sugar, soap, and exercise books. Everything people need.

**Ask:** What do you buy from a shop?

---

# Page 3
![Page 3](images/page-03.png)

Mr. Mukasa sweeps the floor. He makes everything clean and neat. A clean shop welcomes customers.

**Ask:** Do you help sweep at home?

---

# Page 4
![Page 4](images/page-04.png)

The first customer comes. It is Mr. Opio from the village! "I need a new hoe, Mr. Mukasa."

**Ask:** Why does a farmer need a hoe?

---

# Page 5
![Page 5](images/page-05.png)

Mr. Mukasa finds the best hoe. He tells the true price. He never cheats his customers.

**Ask:** Why is it wrong to cheat?

---

# Page 6
![Page 6](images/page-06.png)

A mother comes with her children. "We need school supplies." Mr. Mukasa helps them find pens and notebooks.

**Ask:** What do you need for school?

---

# Page 7
![Page 7](images/page-07.png)

Little Kizza, Mr. Mukasa's son, comes to help. He learns to count the money. One day, he will run a shop too.

**Ask:** Do you help your parents with their work?

---

# Page 8
![Page 8](images/page-08.png)

Sometimes customers cannot pay. Mr. Mukasa writes in his book. "Pay me when you can." He is kind.

**Ask:** What does it mean to be kind?

---

# Page 9
![Page 9](images/page-09.png)

At lunch, Mr. Kato stops by. "I need medicine for the hospital." Mr. Mukasa has what he needs.

**Ask:** Who helps the shopkeeper?

---

# Page 10
![Page 10](images/page-10.png)

The sun goes down. Mr. Mukasa counts the day's money. He thanks God for good business.

**Ask:** What do you thank God for?

---

# Page 11
![Page 11](images/page-11.png)

He walks home to Mrs. Mukasa and the children. Dinner is ready. The family is together.

**Ask:** What is your favorite dinner?

---

# Page 12
![Page 12](images/page-12.png)

On Sunday, the Mukasa family sits in the front row at church. Pastor Omoding preaches about faithfulness.

**Ask:** What does "faithful" mean to you?
