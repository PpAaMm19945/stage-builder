# Dedication

For all soldiers who protect our nation and still love their families.

---

# Page 1
![Page 1](images/page-01.png)

It is very early. Mr. Emukule puts on his uniform. Green shirt, green trousers, strong black boots. He is a soldier.

**Ask:** What color does a soldier wear?

---

# Page 2
![Page 2](images/page-02.png)

Mrs. Emukule holds his hand. "Come back safely." Little Okello looks up at his strong Papa with big eyes.

**Ask:** Do you say a prayer when someone leaves?

---

# Page 3
![Page 3](images/page-03.png)

Mr. Emukule goes to the barracks. Other soldiers are there. They exercise together. "One! Two! Three!"

**Ask:** Do you like to exercise?

---

# Page 4
![Page 4](images/page-04.png)

Today they will practice marching. Left, right, left, right. Every soldier moves together. They are a team.

**Ask:** Can you march in place?

---

# Page 5
![Page 5](images/page-05.png)

The commander speaks. "Soldiers, we protect our people. We keep Uganda safe." Mr. Emukule stands tall.

**Ask:** Who keeps your town safe?

---

# Page 6
![Page 6](images/page-06.png)

Soldiers do many jobs. Some guard important buildings. Some help when there are floods. Mr. Emukule helps wherever he is needed.

**Ask:** How do people help during hard times?

---

# Page 7
![Page 7](images/page-07.png)

Today, soldiers visit a school. The children cheer! Mr. Emukule shakes hands with them. "Study hard," he says.

**Ask:** Have soldiers ever visited your school?

---

# Page 8
![Page 8](images/page-08.png)

Being a soldier is hard. Sometimes Mr. Emukule is away from home for many days. He misses his family very much.

**Ask:** Do you miss someone when they are far away?

---

# Page 9
![Page 9](images/page-09.png)

But today, Mr. Emukule comes home! Okello runs to him. "Papa! Papa!" They hug for a long, long time.

**Ask:** How do you feel when someone you love comes home?

---

# Page 10
![Page 10](images/page-10.png)

Mrs. Emukule has cooked his favorite food. The family sits together. They laugh. They talk. They are happy.

**Ask:** What is your favorite food?

---

# Page 11
![Page 11](images/page-11.png)

Mr. Emukule reads to Okello before bed. Even soldiers are gentle with their children.

**Ask:** Does your father read to you?

---

# Page 12
![Page 12](images/page-12.png)

On Sunday, the Emukule family is at church. Pastor Omoding prays for soldiers. "Lord, protect those who protect us."

**Ask:** Who do you pray for?
