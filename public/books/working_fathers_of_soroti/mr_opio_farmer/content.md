# Dedication

For all the farmers who grow the food that feeds our families.

---

# Page 1
![Page 1](images/page-01.png)

The stars are still in the sky. Mr. Opio wakes up very early. Farmers start before the sun.

**Ask:** Do you like to wake up early?

---

# Page 2
![Page 2](images/page-02.png)

Mrs. Opio brings him hot porridge. Little Etyang is still sleeping. The farm is calling.

**Ask:** What does your family eat in the morning?

---

# Page 3
![Page 3](images/page-03.png)

Mr. Opio walks to the garden. He carries his hoe. The soil is good. The rains have come.

**Ask:** What grows in a garden?

---

# Page 4
![Page 4](images/page-04.png)

He digs and plants seeds. Beans, maize, and groundnuts. Each seed is a promise.

**Ask:** Have you ever planted a seed?

---

# Page 5
![Page 5](images/page-05.png)

The sun grows hot. Mr. Opio's back aches. But he keeps working. Good food needs hard work.

**Ask:** What happens when you work very hard?

---

# Page 6
![Page 6](images/page-06.png)

After many weeks, the crops are ready! Green leaves, golden maize, brown groundnuts. God has blessed the farm.

**Ask:** What color is maize when it is ready?

---

# Page 7
![Page 7](images/page-07.png)

Mr. Opio loads his bicycle. He will ride to Soroti market. The town needs food from the village.

**Ask:** How do you carry heavy things?

---

# Page 8
![Page 8](images/page-08.png)

At the market, he sells his crops. "Very fresh! Very good!" A woman buys beans. A man buys maize.

**Ask:** What do people sell at the market?

---

# Page 9
![Page 9](images/page-09.png)

Mr. Opio counts his shillings. He earned money for his hard work. He will buy new shoes for Etyang.

**Ask:** What would you buy at the market?

---

# Page 10
![Page 10](images/page-10.png)

He rides home as the sun sets. The village road is dusty but familiar. Home is near.

**Ask:** What road do you walk on to get home?

---

# Page 11
![Page 11](images/page-11.png)

Etyang runs to meet Papa. "Did you sell everything?" Mr. Opio smiles and shows the new shoes.

**Ask:** What makes you happy when your father comes home?

---

# Page 12
![Page 12](images/page-12.png)

On Sunday, the Opio family travels to Soroti for church. They see Mr. Kato and Mr. Okello. They all worship together.

**Ask:** Who do you see at church?
