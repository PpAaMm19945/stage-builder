# Dedication

For all the builders who create homes where families can live safely.

---

# Page 1
![Page 1](images/page-01.png)

Mr. Ejoku stretches his strong arms. Today he will build. He is a mason, a builder of homes.

**Ask:** What is your home made of?

---

# Page 2
![Page 2](images/page-02.png)

Mrs. Ejoku gives him breakfast and water for the day. Little Imukon wants to come. "When you are bigger," Papa says.

**Ask:** Do you want to help build things?

---

# Page 3
![Page 3](images/page-03.png)

Mr. Ejoku walks to the building site. A new house is going up. The foundation is already laid.

**Ask:** What is a foundation?

---

# Page 4
![Page 4](images/page-04.png)

He mixes cement with sand and water. It becomes thick and grey. This holds the bricks together.

**Ask:** Have you ever mixed anything?

---

# Page 5
![Page 5](images/page-05.png)

Brick by brick, row by row. Mr. Ejoku works carefully. Each brick must be straight. Good work takes time.

**Ask:** Why is it important to work carefully?

---

# Page 6
![Page 6](images/page-06.png)

His helper brings more bricks. They work together. "Pass me the trowel, please." Teamwork makes the work easier.

**Ask:** Who do you work with as a team?

---

# Page 7
![Page 7](images/page-07.png)

The sun is hot. Mr. Ejoku wipes his face. He drinks water. Then back to work. The wall grows taller.

**Ask:** What do you do when you are tired?

---

# Page 8
![Page 8](images/page-08.png)

By afternoon, the walls are finished. Tomorrow they will put on the roof. Mr. Ejoku looks at his work. It is good.

**Ask:** Have you ever made something you are proud of?

---

# Page 9
![Page 9](images/page-09.png)

The owner of the house comes to see. "Beautiful work, Mr. Ejoku!" He pays him for the day's work.

**Ask:** What do you get when you work hard?

---

# Page 10
![Page 10](images/page-10.png)

Mr. Ejoku walks home. His muscles ache but he is happy. He built something real today.

**Ask:** What do you feel when you finish a hard job?

---

# Page 11
![Page 11](images/page-11.png)

Imukon runs to meet him. "Papa, did you build a lot?" "Yes, my son. Someday I will teach you."

**Ask:** What do you want to learn from your father?

---

# Page 12
![Page 12](images/page-12.png)

On Sunday, the Ejoku family sits at church. They helped build this church too. Pastor Omoding thanks the builders.

**Ask:** What has your family helped build?
