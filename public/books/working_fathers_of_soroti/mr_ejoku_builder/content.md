# Dedication

For all who build the future with skill and dedication.

---

# Page 1
![Page 1](images/page-01.png)

Mr. Ejoku puts on his yellow hard hat and reflective vest. Today is a big day. He is a foreman on a construction site.

**Ask:** What does a foreman do?

---

# Page 2
![Page 2](images/page-02.png)

Mrs. Ejoku packs his lunch. Little Imukon touches Papa's hard hat. "Can I come?" "When you are bigger," Papa smiles.

**Ask:** Do you like hard hats?

---

# Page 3
![Page 3](images/page-03.png)

Mr. Ejoku rides his motorcycle to the site. A big new building is going up—the Soroti Community Center!

Metal scaffolding covers the walls. Workers are already arriving.

**Ask:** Have you seen a building being built?

---

# Page 4
![Page 4](images/page-04.png)

Mr. Ejoku checks the blueprints. The engineer drew the plans. Now Mr. Ejoku must make sure everything is built correctly.

**Ask:** What is a blueprint?

---

# Page 5
![Page 5](images/page-05.png)

BRRRRR! The concrete mixer spins. Workers pour cement into wheelbarrows. Everything must be mixed just right.

**Ask:** Have you heard a loud machine?

---

# Page 6
![Page 6](images/page-06.png)

"Careful! Straight line!" Mr. Ejoku calls to the masons. They lay blocks in perfect rows. One mistake and the wall will be crooked.

**Ask:** Why is it important to work carefully?

---

# Page 7
![Page 7](images/page-07.png)

Mr. Ejoku walks around, checking everything. He helps one worker measure. He shows another how to mix the plaster.

A good foreman teaches his team.

**Ask:** Have you ever taught someone something?

---

# Page 8
![Page 8](images/page-08.png)

The sun is hot. Mr. Ejoku calls for a water break. "Drink, everyone! Then we continue."

He wipes his brow. The walls are growing taller.

**Ask:** What do you do when work is hard?

---

# Page 9
![Page 9](images/page-09.png)

The engineer arrives in her small car to inspect the work. She checks the measurements and nods.

"Excellent work, Mr. Ejoku. The walls are perfectly straight."

**Ask:** How do you feel when someone says you did well?

---

# Page 10
![Page 10](images/page-10.png)

At the end of the day, Mr. Ejoku pays his workers. He counts the money carefully. "Thank you for your hard work. See you tomorrow."

**Ask:** Why is it important to pay workers fairly?

---

# Page 11
![Page 11](images/page-11.png)

Mr. Ejoku rides home as the sun sets. His body is tired but his heart is full. The building is one day closer to finished.

Imukon runs to meet him. "Papa! How tall is it now?"

**Ask:** What makes you proud?

---

# Page 12
![Page 12](images/page-12.png)

On Sunday, the Ejoku family worships at church. Pastor Omoding says, "Thank you to all who are building our Community Center. You build more than walls—you build hope."

**Ask:** How can building something help others?
