For all those who serve to keep our communities safe.

Mr. Odong walks to the police station. Other officers are there. They greet each other.

# Page 4

Today, Mr. Odong patrols the market. He walks slowly. He watches carefully. He keeps everyone safe.

**Ask:** What does "patrol" mean?

# Page 5
![Page 5](images/page-05.png)

A little boy is crying. He is lost! Mr. Odong kneels down. "What is your name? Let us find your mother."

**Ask:** What would you do if you were lost?

---

# Page 6
![Page 6](images/page-06.png)

They find the boy's mother near Mr. Mukasa's shop. She hugs her son. "Thank you, Officer!"

**Ask:** How do you say thank you?

# Page 7
![Page 7](images/page-07.png)

Two men are arguing. Their voices are loud. Mr. Odong walks over calmly. "Let us talk peacefully."

**Ask:** What is the right way to solve a problem?

---

# Page 8
![Page 8](images/page-08.png)

The men calm down. They shake hands. Mr. Odong smiles. Peace is better than fighting.

**Ask:** Have you ever helped two people stop arguing?

# Page 9
![Page 9](images/page-09.png)

In the afternoon, Mr. Odong helps children cross the road. He stops the cars. Safety first!

**Ask:** How do you cross the road safely?

# Page 10
