For all the fathers who work with their hands to provide for their families.

![Page 2](images/page-02.png)

# Page 3
![Page 3](images/page-03.png)

Mr. Okello walks to his garage on Main Street. His tools hang on the wall, neat and clean.

**Ask:** What tools does a mechanic use?

# Page 4
![Page 4](images/page-04.png)

A farmer drives in with a broken truck. "Mr. Okello, I need to carry maize to the market!"

**Ask:** Why do farmers need trucks?

# Page 5
![Page 5](images/page-05.png)

Mr. Okello looks under the truck. He finds the problem. A broken belt. He can fix it!

**Ask:** Have you ever fixed something that was broken?

# Page 6
![Page 6](images/page-06.png)

He works carefully. His hands are strong. He does not rush. Good work takes time.

**Ask:** Why is it important to work carefully?

# Page 7

A boda-boda driver comes next. "My motorcycle makes a strange noise!" Mr. Okello listens and thinks.

# Page 8
![Page 8](images/page-08.png)

"It will cost five thousand shillings," Mr. Okello says. He always tells the true price. He is honest.

**Ask:** Why is it important to be honest?
