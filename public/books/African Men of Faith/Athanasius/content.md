# Dedication

To all the little warriors who stand for the truth, even when they stand alone.

---

# Page 1
![Page 1](images/Page 1.png)

Long, long ago, in the ancient city of Alexandria, Egypt, a brave little boy named Athanasius was born.

Even as a child, Athanasius loved to learn about God and His wonderful Word. He especially loved to hear about Jesus, God's Son, who came to earth to save us!

---

# Page 2
![Page 2](images/Page 2.png)

As Athanasius grew, he became a wise and thoughtful young man. He learned that some people were teaching confusing things about Jesus. They said Jesus was not truly God, but only a created being, like us.

Athanasius knew this was wrong! The Bible taught him that Jesus is fully God, just like the Father and the Holy Spirit. This truth was like a strong, tall tower in his heart.

---

# Page 3
![Page 3](images/page 3.png)

Athanasius knew he had to stand for the truth, even if it meant standing alone. He remembered what the Bible said: "Do not be conformed to this world, but be transformed by the renewal of your mind."

He began to speak out, explaining clearly from the Scriptures that Jesus is indeed God of very God!

---

# Page 4
![Page 4](images/Page 4.png)

Many powerful leaders didn't like what Athanasius was saying. They wanted him to be quiet.

But Athanasius, who was small in stature, had a giant's courage! He believed God's Word more than he believed the opinions of men.

He stood "Contra Mundum" – which means "Against the World" – for the truth about Jesus!

---

# Page 5
![Page 5](images/Page 5.png)

Because he wouldn't stop speaking the truth, Athanasius was sent away from his home in Alexandria again and again! He was exiled five different times!

Imagine having to pack up and leave your home because you believed in Jesus with all your heart!

---

# Page 6
![Page 6](images/Page 6.png)

During his long exiles, Athanasius never gave up. He wrote letters, studied the Bible, and prayed.

He knew that God was with him, even when he felt alone. He knew that God's truth would always win in the end.

---

# Page 7
![Page 7](images/Page 7.png)

Sometimes, he would hide in deserts or remote villages, always finding ways to teach and encourage God's people.

Even when it was dangerous, Athanasius kept sharing the good news that Jesus is Lord and God!

---

# Page 8
![Page 8](images/Page 8.png)

Athanasius faced many troubles, but he always trusted God. He believed that even when the world seemed against him, God's Word was true, and God's strength would carry him through.

His faith was like a sturdy ship in a stormy sea!

![Page 9](images/Page 9.png)

When he was finally allowed to return to Alexandria, the people rejoiced! They loved Athanasius, their brave bishop, who had fought so hard for the truth.

He taught them that Jesus, our Savior, is truly God, and that through Him, we can have eternal life!

# A Note for Parents & Teachers

This book tells the story of one of the most important figures in church history. Here are some themes to discuss:

- **The Deity of Christ:** Athanasius fought against the idea that Jesus was a created being. He taught that Jesus is fully God (of the same substance as the Father). This is crucial because only God can save us from our sins.
- **Contra Mundum (Against the World):** Athanasius was often the only one standing for the truth when powerful leaders and even other bishops compromised. This teaches children that truth is not determined by popularity or power, but by God's Word.
- **Perseverance in Suffering:** Despite being exiled five times, Athanasius never gave up. His life encourages us to trust in God's providence even when we are chased away, lonely, or misunderstood.
