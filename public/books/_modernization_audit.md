# Book Series Modernization Audit + Image Prompts

## The Problem

The books risk portraying an **outdated, "Western-stereotype-of-Africa"** view:
- Mud huts instead of plastered brick homes
- Hand tools instead of modern equipment
- Rural isolation instead of connected communities
- Old-fashioned professions instead of 2026 reality

## Modern Uganda (2026) Visual Language

### Architecture
| ❌ Avoid | ✓ Modern Reality |
|----------|------------------|
| Mud/thatch huts | Plastered brick homes, painted walls |
| Bare cement floors | Tiled floors (inside) |
| Small windows | Large glazed windows with metal frames |
| Grass roofs | Pre-painted iron sheets (red, blue, green) |
| No electricity | Solar panels, power lines, light fixtures |

### Vehicles
| ❌ Avoid | ✓ Modern Reality |
|----------|------------------|
| Ox carts only | Boda-bodas (100-150cc motorcycles) |
| Rustic bicycles | Modern mountain bikes |
| No cars | Toyota sedans (Premio, Fielder), SUVs (Land Cruiser, RAV4) |
| Old buses | Modern matatus, HiAce "drones" |

### Equipment & Technology
| ❌ Avoid | ✓ Modern Reality |
|----------|------------------|
| Only hand tools | Power tools, generators |
| No phones | Smartphones (even villagers have them) |
| Charcoal stoves only | Gas cookers, charcoal + modern kitchens |
| No modern medicine | Stethoscopes, IV drips, oxygen tanks |

### Book 7: Mr. Ejoku the Builder 🔴 MAJOR UPDATE NEEDED
**Current:** Mason laying bricks by hand, mixing cement manually
**This is the most "old-school" book and needs significant modernization.**

**Updates needed:**
- Role upgrade: Mr. Ejoku should be a **Foreman (Pota)** or **Site Supervisor** — not just a laborer
- Or alternatively: he can be an **experienced mason** who leads a crew
- Modern site elements:
  - Scaffolding (metal poles, not just wooden)
  - Concrete mixer (gasoline-powered)
  - Safety gear (hard hats, reflective vests)
  - Plastered walls, iron sheet roofing
  - Maybe building something impressive: a multi-story building, a community hall
- Hierarchy shown: foreman directing workers, engineer visiting

**CONTENT REVISION:**

Original:
> "Mr. Ejoku stretches his strong arms. Today he will build. He is a mason, a builder of homes."

Revised:
> "Mr. Ejoku puts on his hard hat and reflective vest. Today there is a big job. He is a foreman on a construction site."

**Image prompt template:**
```
A cartoon illustration in a friendly, warm style with bold outlines.
The scene is a modern construction site in Soroti, Uganda.
A multi-story building is being constructed with metal scaffolding on the outside.
An orange concrete mixer sits on the ground, workers in hard hats carry materials.
Mr. Ejoku is the foreman: he wears a yellow reflective vest, hard hat, holds a clipboard or blueprint.
The walls are plastered brick, the roof frame is metal (ready for iron sheets).
[Page-specific details...]
```

## SANYU'S GROWING HEART SERIES — Audit

These books are more character-focused, but settings should be modern.

### Base Setting Elements (For All Books)
**Sanyu's Family Home:**
```
A comfortable rural home in Namasagali village, Uganda.
The house has plastered brick walls painted cream/pink, a red iron sheet roof.
Large windows with curtains, a concrete veranda, swept compound.
Inside: tiled floor, cushioned sofas, curtains, family photos on walls.
```

**Soroti Town (When Visiting):**
```
A bustling trading town with paved main roads.
Shops have painted storefronts, glass displays, and signage.
Boda-bodas and sedans drive past. People in colorful modern clothes.
```
