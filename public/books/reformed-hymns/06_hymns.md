# Hymns 51-60

## 51. Joy to the World

<div class="hymn">

<div class="hymn-verse">
1. Joy to the world, the Lord is come!<br>
Let earth receive her King;<br>
Let every heart prepare Him room,<br>
And heaven and nature sing,<br>
And heaven and nature sing,<br>
And heaven, and heaven, and nature sing.
</div>

<div class="hymn-verse">
2. Joy to the earth, the Savior reigns!<br>
Let men their songs employ;<br>
While fields and floods, rocks, hills and plains<br>
Repeat the sounding joy,<br>
Repeat the sounding joy,<br>
Repeat, repeat, the sounding joy.
</div>

<div class="hymn-verse">
3. No more let sins and sorrows grow,<br>
Nor thorns infest the ground;<br>
He comes to make His blessings flow<br>
Far as the curse is found,<br>
Far as the curse is found,<br>
Far as, far as, the curse is found.
</div>

<div class="hymn-verse">
4. He rules the world with truth and grace,<br>
And makes the nations prove<br>
The glories of His righteousness,<br>
And wonders of His love,<br>
And wonders of His love,<br>
And wonders, wonders, of His love.
</div>

<div class="hymn-author">Isaac Watts, 1719</div>
</div>

## 52. Hark! The Herald Angels Sing

<div class="hymn">

<div class="hymn-verse">
1. Hark! the herald angels sing,<br>
"Glory to the newborn King:<br>
peace on earth, and mercy mild,<br>
God and sinners reconciled!"<br>
Joyful, all ye nations, rise,<br>
join the triumph of the skies;<br>
with th'angelic hosts proclaim,<br>
"Christ is born in Bethlehem!"
</div>

<div class="hymn-chorus">
Chorus:<br>
Hark! the herald angels sing,<br>
"Glory to the newborn King"
</div>

<div class="hymn-verse">
2. Christ, by highest heaven adored,<br>
Christ, the everlasting Lord,<br>
late in time behold him come,<br>
offspring of the Virgin's womb:<br>
veiled in flesh the Godhead see;<br>
hail th'incarnate Deity,<br>
pleased with us in flesh to dwell,<br>
Jesus, our Immanuel.
</div>

<div class="hymn-verse">
3. Hail the heaven-born Prince of Peace!<br>
Hail the Sun of Righteousness!<br>
Light and life to all he brings,<br>
risen with healing in his wings.<br>
Mild he lays his glory by,<br>
born that man no more may die,<br>
born to raise the sons of earth,<br>
born to give them second birth.
</div>

<div class="hymn-author">Charles Wesley, 1739</div>
</div>

## 53. O Come, All Ye Faithful

<div class="hymn">

<div class="hymn-verse">
1. O come, all ye faithful, joyful and triumphant,<br>
O come ye, O come ye to Bethlehem;<br>
come and behold him born the King of angels;
</div>

<div class="hymn-chorus">
Chorus:<br>
O come, let us adore him,<br>
O come, let us adore him,<br>
O come, let us adore him,<br>
Christ the Lord.
</div>

<div class="hymn-verse">
2. God of God, Light of Light,<br>
lo! he abhors not the Virgin's womb;<br>
very God, begotten, not created;
</div>

<div class="hymn-verse">
3. Sing, choirs of angels, sing in exultation,<br>
sing, all ye citizens of heaven above;<br>
glory to God in the highest;
</div>

<div class="hymn-author">John F. Wade, 1743; tr. Frederick Oakeley, 1841</div>
</div>

## 54. Silent Night

<div class="hymn">

<div class="hymn-verse">
1. Silent night, holy night,<br>
all is calm, all is bright<br>
round yon virgin mother and child.<br>
Holy infant, so tender and mild,<br>
sleep in heavenly peace,<br>
sleep in heavenly peace.
</div>

<div class="hymn-verse">
2. Silent night, holy night,<br>
shepherds quake at the sight;<br>
glories stream from heaven afar,<br>
heavenly hosts sing Alleluia!<br>
Christ the Savior is born,<br>
Christ the Savior is born!
</div>

<div class="hymn-verse">
3. Silent night, holy night,<br>
Son of God, love's pure light;<br>
radiant beams from thy holy face<br>
with the dawn of redeeming grace,<br>
Jesus, Lord, at thy birth,<br>
Jesus, Lord, at thy birth.
</div>

<div class="hymn-author">Joseph Mohr, 1818; tr. John F. Young, 1863</div>
</div>

## 55. Angels We Have Heard on High

<div class="hymn">

<div class="hymn-verse">
1. Angels we have heard on high<br>
Sweetly singing o'er the plains,<br>
And the mountains in reply<br>
Echoing their joyous strains.
</div>

<div class="hymn-chorus">
Chorus:<br>
Gloria, in excelsis Deo!<br>
Gloria, in excelsis Deo!
</div>

<div class="hymn-verse">
2. Shepherds, why this jubilee?<br>
Why your joyous strains prolong?<br>
What the gladsome tidings be<br>
Which inspire your heavenly song?
</div>

<div class="hymn-verse">
3. Come to Bethlehem and see<br>
Him whose birth the angels sing,<br>
Come, adore on bended knee,<br>
Christ the Lord, the newborn King.
</div>

<div class="hymn-author">French Carol; tr. James Chadwick, 1862</div>
</div>

## 56. The First Noel

<div class="hymn">

<div class="hymn-verse">
1. The first Noel the angel did say<br>
was to certain poor shepherds in fields as they lay;<br>
in fields where they lay keeping their sheep,<br>
on a cold winter's night that was so deep.
</div>

<div class="hymn-chorus">
Chorus:<br>
Noel, Noel, Noel, Noel,<br>
born is the King of Israel.
</div>

<div class="hymn-verse">
2. They looked up and saw a star<br>
shining in the east, beyond them far;<br>
and to the earth it gave great light,<br>
and so it continued both day and night.
</div>

<div class="hymn-verse">
3. And by the light of that same star<br>
three wise men came from country far;<br>
to seek for a king was their intent,<br>
and to follow the star wherever it went.
</div>

<div class="hymn-author">Traditional English Carol</div>
</div>

## 57. O Little Town of Bethlehem

<div class="hymn">

<div class="hymn-verse">
1. O little town of Bethlehem,<br>
how still we see thee lie!<br>
Above thy deep and dreamless sleep<br>
the silent stars go by.<br>
Yet in thy dark streets shineth<br>
the everlasting Light;<br>
the hopes and fears of all the years<br>
are met in thee tonight.
</div>

<div class="hymn-verse">
2. For Christ is born of Mary,<br>
and gathered all above,<br>
while mortals sleep, the angels keep<br>
their watch of wondering love.<br>
O morning stars, together<br>
proclaim the holy birth,<br>
and praises sing to God the King,<br>
and peace to men on earth.
</div>

<div class="hymn-verse">
3. How silently, how silently,<br>
the wondrous gift is giv'n!<br>
So God imparts to human hearts<br>
the blessings of his heav'n.<br>
No ear may hear his coming,<br>
but in this world of sin,<br>
where meek souls will receive him, still<br>
the dear Christ enters in.
</div>

<div class="hymn-author">Phillips Brooks, 1868</div>
</div>

## 58. Away in a Manger

<div class="hymn">

<div class="hymn-verse">
1. Away in a manger, no crib for a bed,<br>
the little Lord Jesus laid down his sweet head.<br>
The stars in the bright sky looked down where he lay,<br>
the little Lord Jesus asleep on the hay.
</div>

<div class="hymn-verse">
2. The cattle are lowing, the baby awakes,<br>
but little Lord Jesus, no crying he makes.<br>
I love thee, Lord Jesus! Look down from the sky,<br>
and stay by my cradle till morning is nigh.
</div>

<div class="hymn-verse">
3. Be near me, Lord Jesus, I ask thee to stay<br>
close by me forever, and love me, I pray.<br>
Bless all the dear children in thy tender care,<br>
and fit us for heaven to live with thee there.
</div>

<div class="hymn-author">Anonymous, 1885</div>
</div>

## 59. What Child Is This?

<div class="hymn">

<div class="hymn-verse">
1. What child is this, who, laid to rest,<br>
on Mary's lap is sleeping?<br>
Whom angels greet with anthems sweet,<br>
while shepherds watch are keeping?
</div>

<div class="hymn-chorus">
Chorus:<br>
This, this is Christ the King,<br>
whom shepherds guard and angels sing:<br>
haste, haste to bring him laud,<br>
the babe, the son of Mary.
</div>

<div class="hymn-verse">
2. Why lies he in such mean estate<br>
where ox and ass are feeding?<br>
Good Christian, fear: for sinners here<br>
the silent Word is pleading.
</div>

<div class="hymn-verse">
3. So bring him incense, gold, and myrrh,<br>
come, peasant, king, to own him.<br>
The King of kings salvation brings;<br>
let loving hearts enthrone him.
</div>

<div class="hymn-author">William C. Dix, 1865</div>
</div>

## 60. God Rest Ye Merry, Gentlemen

<div class="hymn">

<div class="hymn-verse">
1. God rest ye merry, gentlemen,<br>
let nothing you dismay,<br>
remember Christ our Savior<br>
was born on Christmas Day<br>
to save us all from Satan's power<br>
when we were gone astray.
</div>

<div class="hymn-chorus">
Chorus:<br>
O tidings of comfort and joy, comfort and joy;<br>
O tidings of comfort and joy.
</div>

<div class="hymn-verse">
2. From God our heavenly Father<br>
a blessed angel came;<br>
and unto certain shepherds<br>
brought tidings of the same:<br>
how that in Bethlehem was born<br>
the Son of God by name.
</div>

<div class="hymn-author">Traditional English Carol</div>
</div>
